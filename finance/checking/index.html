Notes

Simplii, TD, and CIBC currently have the most straightforward requirements and highest bonus-to-effort ratio for someone without a Canadian job yet lined up. Simplii and Tangerine have no account fee ever, regardless of newcomer status.

All offers require a Canadian address and SIN to open, and most require the account to stay open for 6–12 months to keep the bonus. Deadlines shown are the account-opening deadlines; some offers give additional time after opening to complete the qualifying activity.

Promotions change frequently and are set directly by each bank — always verify current terms on the official bank page before opening an account. This page is updated periodically but may lag behind live changes.
