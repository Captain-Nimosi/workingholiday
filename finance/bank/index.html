Canadian Banks

Canada's banking sector is dominated by six major banks (the 'Big Six'), which together hold about 90% of all banking assets in the country. Two of them also run separate internet-only brands.

Last updated: September 6, 2026
Bank Founded / HQ Notable for Official site
RBC (Royal Bank of Canada) 1864, Toronto Largest bank in Canada, largest branch/ATM network rbcroyalbank.com
TD (Toronto-Dominion Bank) 1855, Toronto Large presence in both Canada and the US td.com
Scotiabank (Bank of Nova Scotia) 1832, Toronto Strongest international presence, especially Latin America scotiabank.com

BMO (Bank of Montreal) 1817, Montreal / Toronto Canada's oldest bank bmo.com
CIBC (Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce) 1961, Toronto Smallest of the Big Five, but most aggressive newcomer/promo offers cibc.com
Simplii Financial CIBC 계열 2017, Toronto Branchless, uses CIBC ATM network simplii.com
National Bank of Canada 1859, Montreal 6th largest, dominant mainly in Quebec nbc.ca

Notes

Simplii and Tangerine are internet-only divisions of CIBC and Scotiabank respectively — they use their parent bank's ATM network but operate as separate brands with their own accounts, cards, and promotions.

National Bank is included here for completeness but is less relevant for someone settling outside Quebec.

Bank rankings and features may change over time — always check the official site for current information.
