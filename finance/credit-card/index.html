Simplii and Tangerine issue their own credit cards independently — they are not the same product as their parent bank's card, but a newcomer with no Canadian credit history can typically apply for either.

Notes

CIBC and TD currently offer the best combination of low effort and solid welcome bonus for a first Canadian credit card. All Big Five banks accept newcomers with no Canadian credit history through dedicated programs (RBC Newcomer Advantage, TD New to Canada, CIBC Newcomer, BMO NewStart, Scotiabank StartRight), typically allowing credit limits up to $10,000–15,000 based on income rather than credit history.

A credit card usually needs to be opened with an existing chequing account at the same bank, so it often makes sense to combine a checking account promotion with a credit card promotion at the same bank.

Promotions and approval criteria change frequently and are set directly by each bank — always verify current terms on the official bank page before applying. This page is updated periodically but may lag behind live changes.
