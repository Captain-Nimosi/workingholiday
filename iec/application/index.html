Create your IEC profile

Create and submit your IEC profile and choose the Working Holiday pool. You must be eligible for IEC before submitting your profile.

Wait for an Invitation to Apply

After submitting your profile, you remain in the IEC pool until you receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA), your profile expires, or the season closes.

05

Give your biometrics

After you submit your application, you may receive a Biometrics Instruction Letter (BIL) in your IRCC account. If required, you must give your fingerprints and photo in person within 30 days of receiving the BIL.
