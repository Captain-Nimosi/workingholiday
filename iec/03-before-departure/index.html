Before Departure

For Korean citizens participating in the IEC Working Holiday program.
Based on information provided by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
Last updated: August 26, 2026

01

Before departure

Documents

Travel arrangements

Before making commitments

Wait until your IEC application is approved before making major travel, insurance, employment or accommodation commitments.

Check the official IRCC guidance.

↓
02

At the border

Documents to present

Proof of funds

Your bank statement should be issued no more than 1 week before your departure and show enough funds to support yourself for the first 3 months in Canada.

The current IRCC requirement is the equivalent of CAN$2,500.

Check the official proof of funds requirements.

Health insurance

Your insurance should cover medical care, hospitalization and repatriation for your entire stay.

If your insurance is valid for less than your intended stay, your work permit may be issued only until the insurance expiry date.

03

After entry

Review your work permit

Check your work permit carefully before leaving the port of entry.

If there is a mistake

Tell the border services officer immediately before leaving the port of entry.

Check the official IRCC instructions.
